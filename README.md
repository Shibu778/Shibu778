## 🔬 Computational Materials Scientist & Developer

I am a passionate computational materials scientist specializing in **first-principles investigations of point defects** in solid-state structures. My work bridges condensed matter physics, quantum technologies, and software engineering to explore and engineer defect centers for next-generation quantum applications.

* **Core Focus:** Electronic structure modeling, electron-phonon coupling analytics, and photoluminescence (PL) lineshape predictions.
* **Technical Frontiers:** Scientific software development, machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs), and high-throughput materials discovery.

> 💡 *"In the lattice of knowledge, every defect is a doorway to discovery."*

---

## 🤝 Areas for Collaboration & Opportunities

I am always eager to team up with academic researchers, open-source developers, and industry teams on:
* **Quantum Defect Modeling:** High-fidelity ab initio calculations of color centers and spin-photon interfaces.
* **Scientific Software Engineering:** Designing scalable, extensible, and code-decoupled materials physics packages.
* **AI/ML for Materials:** Accelerating phonon and thermodynamic trait predictions via machine learning frameworks.
